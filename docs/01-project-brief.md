# 01 — Project Brief

**Status:** Draft — based on presentation

## Project overview
UniNest is a digital platform intended to help university students in Lahore find suitable hostels and apartments near their universities. It aims to make accommodation search easier through verified listings, clear property information, filters, and a convenient booking process.

## Problem
The presentation identifies several problems:
- Listings can be unverified or misleading.
- Students may struggle to find accommodation near their university.
- Information is scattered across social media, agents, and word of mouth.
- Searching, visiting, and negotiating can take considerable time.

## Proposed solution
A centralized website where students can discover, compare, and book accommodation, with property information and verification handled through a defined process.

## Target users
- University and college students, including students relocating to Lahore
- Hostel and apartment owners/managers
- UniNest administrators (proposed operational role)

The presentation identifies Lahore as the initial geographic focus and names UOL, UVAS, GCU, Kinnaird College, and the University of Education as examples of university areas to consider.

## Value proposition
- Student-focused accommodation discovery
- Verified listing information
- Search by price, location/proximity, room type, rating, and facilities
- Convenient booking experience

## Business model
- **Presentation-derived:** 20% commission on each booking.
- **Planned:** Hostel listing fees.
- **Future:** Featured listings and promotions.
- The commission base, payer, timing, refunds, and settlement process remain open decisions.

## Initial scope proposal
Build a responsive web MVP focused on Lahore, property listings, search and filters, a verification workflow, and booking management.

## Not committed for the MVP
Payment gateway, online payouts, live chat, map integration, reviews, advanced analytics, AI recommendations, listing fees, featured promotions, and native mobile apps require prioritization and confirmation.

## Success measures mentioned in the presentation
- Website traffic
- Number of bookings
- Conversion rate from ads
- Listing growth

## Risks to plan for
- Misleading or fraudulent listings
- Out-of-date availability or prices
- Booking conflicts
- Privacy of owner/property verification documents
- Cancellation and payment disputes
- Building trust before a large verified inventory exists

## Current assumptions
- React frontend and FastAPI backend are the intended technology choices.
- Development is initially undertaken by one developer.
- The project scope will evolve during development.
